POLICY / TRANSPARENCY & CONTROL

Privacy policy

Nicholas Liang Creative Tech respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be handled when you visit this portfolio or use an immersive experience made by Nicholas Liang, why it is used, and how to request its deletion.

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 7, 2026

Which experience? This portfolio is a static information website. Individual VR/AR projects can have different features and data practices. Where a project has its own in-product privacy notice or store listing, that notice describes the project-specific details; the categories below explain the types of information an experience may process when its features require them.

1. Who is responsible

This policy covers the portfolio at nicholasliang.com and creative technology experiences developed or published by Nicholas Liang (“we,” “us”). Some gallery links lead to third-party websites or services. Those services are operated by others and their own privacy policies apply when you leave this site.

2. Information the portfolio website handles

The portfolio is provided as a static website. It does not ask visitors to create an account, submit a form, or provide personal information to browse. If you choose to email us, we receive the email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and manage the conversation.

Our hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address, browser/device details, and pages requested in server logs to deliver and protect the website. This site does not intentionally use advertising trackers or a website analytics service.

3. Information an immersive experience may process

Depending on the specific project, device, enabled features, and permissions you grant, an experience may process these general categories:

  • Platform and device information: platform user or application identifiers, device model, operating system, app version, and basic diagnostics needed to run, troubleshoot, or support an experience.
  • Spatial and sensor information: headset or controller pose, hand or eye input, scene/room understanding, anchors, or other environment data when a feature uses the relevant platform capability. These inputs can describe a user’s surroundings. They are used to place or control virtual content and are not used by us to identify a person from their surroundings.
  • In-experience activity and content: settings, progress, interaction events, and content you create or choose to share, where an experience offers those features.
  • Support communications: contact details and details you provide if you ask for help.

An experience only uses platform capabilities relevant to its features and permissions. A project may process information on the device without sending it to us. Where an experience uses Meta Platform SDK or other platform services, the platform may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. Check the relevant project’s store listing and in-product notices for the features and permissions it uses.

4. How information is used

Information described above may be used to provide and operate the requested experience, place and render content, enable gameplay or creation features, remember settings or progress when supported, maintain safety and security, diagnose crashes and performance issues, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information or use spatial data to serve targeted advertising.

5. Storage, sharing, and retention

Some project information stays on your device; other information may be sent to a service only when a feature requires it (for example, cloud saves, multiplayer, or platform functionality). A project may not offer all of these features. We do not disclose information to third parties except to provide a feature you choose, use a platform or service provider that supports the experience, protect the experience and its users, or meet a legal requirement. Those providers handle information under their own terms.

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Local data can generally be removed by uninstalling the experience or clearing its saved data through your device settings. Information held by a platform or third-party provider may need to be deleted through that provider.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can decline optional device permissions in your system settings; some features may then be unavailable. You can request deletion of personal information associated with an experience or your support communications at any time, regardless of where you live, by emailing ningtaku@gmail.com with the subject Privacy and data deletion request. Include the project name and enough information to locate the relevant data. Do not email passwords or sensitive identity documents.

We will acknowledge the request, verify it proportionately where needed to protect your information, and delete information we control within a reasonable period. There is no fee. We may retain a limited record when required by law, to resolve a dispute, or to protect security; if so, we will restrict it to that purpose and retain it only as long as necessary. If the data is held by Meta or another platform provider, we will explain how to contact that provider or use its account privacy controls; we cannot delete data from systems we do not control.

7. Meta platform services

Some projects may be distributed on Meta platforms or use Meta Platform SDK features. Those features can involve information processed by Meta to provide platform functionality. Meta’s handling of that information is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy. We recommend reviewing the particular project’s Meta store listing for platform features and permissions. This policy supplements, and does not replace, Meta’s policy.

8. Children, security, and policy changes

Our portfolio is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. An experience’s age rating and platform requirements apply to that experience. We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the information and services involved, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

We may update this policy as projects and practices change. The updated version will be posted at this permanent URL with a revised date. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact ningtaku@gmail.com.
