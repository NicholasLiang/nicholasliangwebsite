ABOUT / PRACTICE

Technology in service
of human stories.

Nicholas Liang is a creative technologist working across immersive media, real-time 3D, and interactive storytelling. His practice combines technical craft with curiosity about how people experience places, ideas, and one another.

His work includes Chinatown XR, the augmented reality project A Seat at the Table for the Museum of Vancouver, the virtual world Jingle Ville, and Awaken the Giants, featured at Science World's Creative Technology Gallery. He also teaches Blender and web design, helping new makers build confidence with creative tools.
