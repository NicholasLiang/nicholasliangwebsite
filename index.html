About

Nick is a Creative Technologist blending modern technology with compelling narratives. Skilled in computer graphics, game engines, and 3D software, he excels in 3D environment design, shader artistry, and generative art, driven by a passion for creating joyful experiences.

Enthusiastic and experimental, Nick seeks new challenges to expand his skill set. Over the past seven years, he has pioneered diverse storytelling projects, including "Chinatown XR," the AR project "A Seat at the Table" for the Museum of Vancouver, the metaverse world "Jingle Ville," "Awaken the Giants" featured at Science World Creative Technology Gallery, and the location-based AR experience "Melting Zone" in Korea.

Dedicated to educating the next wave of creative technologists, Nick teaches Blender 3D and web design, aligning his students' talents with the needs of future society.
